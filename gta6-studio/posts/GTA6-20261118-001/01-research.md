### Derived time conversions (agent arithmetic, not quoted by any source)
- NZ unlock 00:00 NZDT 19 Nov (UTC+13) = 11:00 UTC 18 Nov = **12:00 CET Rome, 18 Nov**. NZ was already live when this post was created (22:35 Rome).
- Rome (CET, UTC+1) PS5 unlock: 00:00 CET, 19 Nov.
- UK (GMT) PS5 unlock: 00:00 GMT, 19 Nov.
- Xbox Cloud 1am EST (UTC-5) = 06:00 UTC = **07:00 CET Rome, 19 Nov** (cloud only).

## What is NOT verified and must not be said
- That Xbox Series X|S **consoles** unlock at midnight local time (FACT-0004). Rockstar Support could not be opened (503 on four attempts), and the Xbox pages give no console time.
- The "12am GMT global" Xbox pre-load time (FACT-0007).
- "No PC version" / "PC coming later" (FACT-0008). Say only "announced for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S". Do not read the Xbox store's "Windows 10/11" purchase-option text as a PC version.
- Per-time-zone unlocks inside multi-time-zone countries (US, Canada, Australia, Brazil, Mexico). PlayStation says "by country". No page I opened says whether US PS5 unlocks at midnight ET for all or per time zone. Third-party reports of a US West Coast 9pm PT unlock are not evidence.
- Any exact unlock-time table for specific countries other than New Zealand (NZDT). Any other times must be labelled as our own conversion of "midnight local".
- That the 1am ET Xbox Cloud time is the Xbox launch time.
- Any Rockstar fan-content policy terms (not read).
- Leaks, region/VPN tricks to play early, or store-region bypass. Prohibited.

## Risks
- **Misinformation (HIGH):** Stating the Xbox console time without a source, and merging the cloud 1am ET time with the console time. US time-zone behaviour is ambiguous.
- **Timeliness (HIGH):** It is about 22:50 Rome on 18 Nov. NZ and many Asia-Pacific countries are already live, and Rome unlocks at 00:00 CET. The video's usefulness drops sharply within hours. Write it as "where it's live / when it's your turn" and not "coming soon".
- **Copyright (MEDIUM):** The fan-content policy was not read (503). Use original graphics and text. Quoting a short sentence from an official page with attribution is lower risk, but the rights reviewer should confirm. Use no Rockstar key art or trailer clips until the policy is read.
- **Affiliation (LOW-MEDIUM):** Platform logos or store-style UI could imply we are official. Use plain text platform names.
- **Spoilers:** none.

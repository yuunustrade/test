**Note 1 (conversion):** 00:00 NZDT (UTC+13, NZ daylight time is in effect in November) on 19 Nov = 11:00 UTC on 18 Nov = **11:00 GMT UK on 18 Nov** (the UK is on GMT in November). Rome would be 12:00 CET on 18 Nov. This is our own arithmetic, not a source quote, and must be labelled on screen. Only the UK conversion is shown because the UK has a single time zone. No US/Canada/Australia/Brazil/Mexico conversions are shown, because PlayStation says "by country" and no source says how multi-time-zone countries are handled.
**Note 2 (time-zone card):** This card makes no claim about how multi-time-zone countries unlock. It only sends viewers to the official page. It exists so that "your country's midnight" is not misread as a per-time-zone promise.

## 6. Do not say
- Any Xbox Series X|S **console** unlock time, including "midnight local on Xbox" (FACT-0004, unverified).
- Any Xbox pre-load time, including "12am GMT global" (FACT-0007, unverified).
- The Xbox Cloud 1am ET time as an Xbox launch time (FACT-0005 is cloud only, and skipped here).
- "No PC version" or "PC coming later" (FACT-0008, unverified). Don't read the Xbox store's "Windows 10/11" text as a PC version.
- Per-time-zone unlocks inside the US, Canada, Australia, Brazil or Mexico, or "9pm PT" West Coast claims.
- Exact unlock times for any country other than New Zealand (NZDT), unless labelled as our conversion.
- Any Rockstar fan-content policy terms (FACT-0009, unread).
- Leaks, region/VPN tricks to play early, store-region bypass.
- "PS5 exclusive". The game is also announced for Xbox Series X|S. Only say "not on PS4".
- Earlier release dates (fall 2025, 26 May 2026) as if current.
- "Secret", "confirmed", "leaked", "Rockstar admits", "everyone". Use "first" only in the PS5/New Zealand sense the source supports.
- "Coming soon" or "already out" phrasing that ages within hours.
- Any claim that we tested or played the game (news-explainer).
